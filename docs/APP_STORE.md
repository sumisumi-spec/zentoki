審査メモ（英語で貼る）:
```
ZENTOKI is a fasting and meditation timer. No account or sign-in is required.

- All data (fasting/meditation/weight records, settings) is stored locally on the device. Nothing is uploaded.
- Notifications are local notifications scheduled when a fast or meditation starts (goal reached, body-stage reached, meditation finished). They can be tested by starting a 3-minute meditation.
- Sounds are synthesized with Web Audio inside the app; there are no downloaded audio files.
- "Apple Health import" reads a Health export file (export.xml) that the user shares into the app; it is parsed on device and only body-mass records are used. The app does not use HealthKit APIs.
- In-app purchase: "ZENTOKI Plus" (non-consumable, app.zentoki.plus) unlocks additional sounds and breathing patterns. The paywall shows the price, "one-time" wording, a Restore Purchases button, and links to terms and privacy. Free features are not time-limited.
- The "body changes" text shown during a fast is general information with a disclaimer; the app does not provide medical advice or diagnosis.
```

## App のプライバシー（App Privacy）

「データを収集しない（Data Not Collected）」を選択。
- アプリはサーバーを持たず、解析 SDK も広告 SDK も入っていない。
- RevenueCat を有効にした場合: 購入情報（Purchase History）が「App の機能」目的で収集され、ユーザーに紐付かない（識別子は匿名の App User ID）。その場合は「購入」→「App の機能」→「ユーザーに紐付けられない」で登録。
